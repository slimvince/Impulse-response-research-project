# Research Findings

This is the accumulated scientific knowledge layer. It is different from `research-log.md`, which records what happened chronologically.

Do not promote an observation to a finding without recording the evidence, replication level, uncertainty, and confounds. Findings may be weakened, superseded, or unresolved as new experiments arrive.

## Evidence discipline

A descriptive difference in frame-level output is not automatically a finding about the instrument or desired transformation. Findings should, where practical, describe recording-level replication, pitch/register and dynamics conditioning, temporal phase, robustness to analysis parameters, and plausible alternative explanations.
