# E011: Truth-Checked SLB-200 Slicing Iteration

E011 is an isolated revision of the SLB-200 event slices. It uses the original external recording and compares its boundaries against the durable 57-row E008 listener audit by source-time overlap. E008/E009 files and human judgments are unchanged; E011 has a separate review UI/storage key.

## Detector behavior

- Recursive pitch analysis uses 2048-sample windows with a 256-sample hop; pitch splits require nearby envelope-onset evidence.
- Pitch estimates are not certified from zero-padded fragments shorter than a full analysis window.
- Events at most 20 ms are labeled disqualified as too short to represent a bass note.
- Events up to 70 ms are disqualified only when peak is at or below -35 dB and no periodic frames are found. Other low-confidence events remain `pitch_unconfirmed`.
- A narrowly defined adjacent extreme-F0 outlier merge handles the audited brief 400+ Hz estimate followed by a low-bass fundamental; general same-pitch events are not merged.

## Validation against durable E008 labels

The report is generated by `export_and_validate.py` and stored in `validation.json`.

- 24/24 E008 `Use` intervals are contained in one E011 event.
- 22/24 have close interval agreement (at least 90% of the old interval covered, and the old interval covers at least 90% of the E011 event).
- The remaining two are extended E011 intervals over continuous same-pitch activity. This may be a real longer note or a boundary disagreement; the E008 verdict alone does not establish exact note endpoints.
- Of 8 E008 comments explicitly saying silence, 6 matching short slices are marked `disqualified`. Two low-level but periodic-looking cases are absorbed into longer events and remain unresolved; broadening the silence rule would risk rejecting positively judged quiet bass events.
- The other 25 E008 `Disq` labels have no reason text, so they cannot safely supervise a particular automatic rejection rule.

The E003 selected-setting event counts remain `3, 3, 3, 5`, and the synthetic test suite passes. These results validate behavior on this small labeled set, not general polyphony detection or universal note-level accuracy.

## Outputs

- `manifest.json`: event boundaries and quality fields.
- `validation.json`: exact overlap mappings and limitations.
- `run_metadata.json`: source hash, settings, and counts.
- `slices/`: exact detector-boundary mono WAV slices without fades, padding, normalization, or postprocessing.
- `review.html`: independent E011 reviewer.
